Things to Do

Hiking & Pony Trekking

Explore the mountains on foot or on the back of a Basotho pony, with routes for all levels.

Skiing at Afriski

Hit the slopes at Southern Africa's only ski resort during the winter season.

Cultural Village Visits

Learn about Basotho traditions, food and the famous Basotho blanket.

Abseiling at Semonkong

Descend one of the highest single-drop abseils in the world next to the Maletsunyane Falls.
